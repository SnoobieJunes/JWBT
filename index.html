Gone Fishing

The summertime ritual, wake up at dawn, grab some grub, head to the shore, and relax.

The Get Hooked and a coffee, on the counter by 5:30. Or a Tackle Box if it is going to be a long one.

Grab bait

Fatheads and worms out of the tanks in back, plus whatever tackle you forgot at home. Same trip, same counter.

Hit the lake

Out the door before the sun is properly up, the lake less than 5 minutes down the road. Lines cast, coffee still hot. That is the whole idea.

About the Shop

JW Bait & Tackle is a family-run shop on the corner of Cayuga Street and Factory. We sell more than just food or fishing supplies, we care about the community and the people in it.

Come in and order at the counter. Call ahead if you are in a hurry. Ask about our catering!
