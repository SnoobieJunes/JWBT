HOW TO CHANGE THE WEBSITE

Everything on the website that changes is in this one spreadsheet.
You need the Google Sheets app on your phone. Nothing else.

There is no save button and no publish button. You type it here, and
it is on the website. Give it five minutes.

The tabs are along the bottom of the screen: Specials, Hours, Menu,
Coffee. Here is what each one does.


--------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIALS  -  the board on the front page
--------------------------------------------------------------

TO ADD ONE
1. Tap the "Specials" tab at the bottom.
2. Tap the first empty row under the last special.
3. Type the name in the "Item" column.
4. Swipe left and fill in "Description" and "Price".
5. Leave "Day" empty if it runs all week. If it is only one day,
   tap the box and pick the day from the list.

TO TAKE ONE OFF
Press and hold the row number on the left, then Delete row.
Do not just erase the words - delete the whole row.

If this tab is empty the website says "Call us for this week's
specials" with the phone number. That is fine. Nothing looks broken.


--------------------------------------------------------------
HOURS  -  the open/closed sign and the hours list
--------------------------------------------------------------

One row per day. Change the "Open" and "Close" boxes.

Write the time any way you like: 5:30 AM, 5:30am, 2:00 PM, 14:00.
They all work.

CLOSED FOR THE DAY
Put the word "Closed" in both boxes, or leave them both empty.

THE "NOTE" COLUMN
Whatever you type there shows up under the day on the website.
Thursday says "Late - dinner specials". Leave it empty for a
normal day.

This tab also drives the "Open now" / "Closed right now" sign at
the top of every page. It works itself out from the clock, so once
the hours are right here you never touch it again.

If you type something the website cannot read, that day just keeps
the hours it had. It will never tell people you are closed when
you are open.


--------------------------------------------------------------
MENU  -  every item and price on the menu page
--------------------------------------------------------------

TO CHANGE A PRICE
Find the row, tap the "Price" box, type the new price. Done.

Write prices with a dollar sign: $5.00. You can also write things
like "Sm $5.00 / Lg $7.50" or "$1.75 each" and it shows up exactly
the way you type it.

TO ADD AN ITEM
Copy the row above it, then change the words. The important part
is that "Section" and "Group" match the rows around it, because
that is what decides where it lands on the page.

  Section  Breakfast, Lunch or Coffee - which part of the page
  Group    the heading it sits under, like "Sandwiches" or
           "The By Catch". Some lists have no heading; leave it
           empty for those.

TO TAKE AN ITEM OFF
Press and hold the row number, then Delete row.

THE ORDER MATTERS
Items appear on the website in the same order they are in here.
To move something up the page, move its row up.

THE "NOTE" COLUMN
A row with a Note and no Item becomes one of the little grey
boxes, like "Add extra meat to any sandwich: $1.00." Put it in
the same Section and Group as the list it belongs under.


--------------------------------------------------------------
COFFEE  -  the flavor list
--------------------------------------------------------------

One flavor per row. Add a row, type the flavor. Delete a row to
take one off. That is the whole tab.

The coffee prices are not here - they are on the Menu tab, under
Section "Coffee".


--------------------------------------------------------------
THINGS WORTH KNOWING
--------------------------------------------------------------

GIVE IT FIVE MINUTES
Google is slow to pass changes along. If you do not see it right
away that is normal. Check again in five minutes before worrying.

NEVER CHANGE ROW 1
The top row of every tab is the labels: Item, Description, Price,
Day, and so on. If those get changed or deleted, that part of the
website stops updating and goes back to what it said before.

NEVER RENAME A TAB
They have to stay called Specials, Hours, Menu and Coffee.

YOU CANNOT BREAK THE WEBSITE FROM HERE
If anything is wrong in this sheet, the website falls back to what
it said last time. Worst case it is a bit out of date. It never
goes blank and it never shows an error.

ANYONE WITH THE LINK CAN READ THIS SHEET
That is how the website reads it. Do not put anything private in
here - no personal details, no passwords, no supplier prices you
would not want a customer to see.
