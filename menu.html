The Bakery & Deli Case

This changes constantly and is not priced here, because what is in it depends on what got made that morning. Have a look when you come in.

Baked in house: jumbo muffins in rotating flavors — pistachio, pumpkin cheesecake, blueberry, red velvet — along with cream puffs, mini fruit tarts, cheesecake, and pies including apple, strawberry chocolate and apple peach.

Also in the case: fresh fruit, deli meats and cheeses, macaroni, potato and pasta salads, and coleslaw.
